2. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
CoverWorth helps users inventory belongings, attach photos and receipts,
review eBay asking-price estimates, and export inventory reports.
Intended users: Anyone who's interested in managing their personal assets all in one convenient location.

4. REQUIRED TEST SCENARIOS
Before adding an item, sign in and create a category if your inventory has
none. On Add Item, select Manage Categories, then New Category. Create
a category such as Electronics and select it when adding the test item.

Scenario 1: Register and add an inventory item
1. Open the hosted app, select Register, and create a test account. Sign in.
2. Open Inventory and select Add Item. Enter Dell P2422H Monitor as the
   name, Dell as the brand, P2422H as the model number, Used as the
   condition, and $75.00 as the purchase price. Complete any other
   required fields and save.
3. Refresh the page and reopen the item.
Expected result: The item is still in the inventory with the same name,
brand, model number, condition, and purchase price.

Scenario 2: Preview and review a valuation
1. On the hosted Valuations page, select the Dell monitor from Scenario 1
   and click Update.
2. If an estimate appears, check the amount, range, and confidence. Click on "Accept $$.$$."
   Next, click on the 3 dots next to "Update" for that listing and select "Valuation History." 
   Open the dropdown to review the comparisons and sources. Open a few listings and check that 
   they are for used P2422H monitors. Flag any accessory-only listing, such as a monitor stand.
3. Check that the saved value stays the same while previewing. Select Accept
   and open Valuation History to confirm the estimate was saved.
Expected result: The preview explains the estimate using comparable
listings. Accept saves it in valuation history. Live prices and counts vary.
If too few listings qualify, record the message and verify that the saved
value stays the same.

Scenario 3: Upload a photo and verify it stays attached
1. Open the Dell monitor from Scenario 1 in Inventory.
2. Edit the item, upload a sample JPG or PNG, and save.
3. Sign out, sign back in, and reopen the item.
4. Open the uploaded photo and confirm it’s the one you selected.
Expected result: The photo remains attached to the Dell monitor after 
signing back in and opens successfully. Use a sample image without personal information.
